# Frameworks And Positioning

Purpose: detailed copywriting frameworks, headline formulas, positioning checks, and value proposition prompts.

## Headline Analysis

Run the 5-second test: would a new visitor understand what the company does and who it serves within 5 seconds of reading the headline?

Score headline options on:

- Clarity: meaning is immediate.
- Specificity: numbers, outcomes, timeframes, or concrete nouns.
- Relevance: speaks to the audience's primary pain or desire.
- Differentiation: separates the offer from alternatives.
- Emotion: creates recognition, desire, curiosity, or relief.

## PAS: Problem, Agitate, Solve

Best for pain-led direct response pages.

```text
Problem: [State the pain point]
Agitate: [Make the pain feel urgent and recognizable]
Solve: [Present the product as the solution]
Headline: "Stop [pain]. Start [desired outcome] with [product]."
```

## AIDA: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action

Best for attention-led pages where the audience needs fast orientation.

```text
Attention: [Surprising fact or bold but supportable claim]
Interest: [Why this matters to the reader]
Desire: [What life or work looks like after using this]
Action: [What to do next]
Headline: "[Supportable claim] - [specific outcome] in [timeframe]."
```

## Before-After-Bridge

Best for transformation-driven products.

```text
Before: [Current painful state]
After: [Desired future state]
Bridge: [How the product connects the two]
Headline: "From [before state] to [after state] - [product] shows the path."
```

## 4U Framework

Best for short headlines, ad lines, and email subject lines.

```text
Useful: [What benefit does it provide?]
Ultra-specific: [Can numbers, timeframes, or concrete nouns be added?]
Unique: [What angle is distinct?]
Urgent: [Why act now, if the urgency is true?]
Headline: "[Specific audience] use [product] to [specific outcome] - [honest urgency or reason to act]."
```

## Positioning Prompts

- Who is this for and who is it not for?
- What costly pain, missed opportunity, or desired identity drives action?
- What mechanism makes this solution different?
- What proof exists today?
- What objection blocks conversion?
- What action should the reader take next?

## Swipe-File Labeling

For every generated headline or concept, include:

- Framework used.
- Audience.
- Primary benefit.
- Proof requirement.
- Risk or compliance note when relevant.

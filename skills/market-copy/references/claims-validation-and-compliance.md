# Claims Validation And Compliance

Purpose: detailed evidence, compliance, and validation checks for ethical persuasion.

## Evidence Rules

- Specific claims need a source: analytics, product data, customer research, case study, public report, or user-provided fact.
- If no source exists, convert the claim into a placeholder, such as `[specific result]`.
- Testimonials require real attribution or must be presented as a placeholder.
- Logos, awards, certifications, partnerships, press mentions, and user counts must be real.
- Guarantees, free trials, refund language, and cancellation terms must match the actual offer.

## Unsupported Claim Patterns

Flag and rewrite:

- "Increase revenue by 50%" without source data.
- "Trusted by leading teams" without customer proof.
- "Only 3 spots left" without live capacity evidence.
- "Best in the market" without a defensible comparison.
- "Risk-free" without real refund or cancellation terms.
- "Guaranteed results" in health, finance, legal, employment, credit, housing, or trading contexts.

## Regulated Or Sensitive Categories

Recommend review for:

- Financial performance, investing, crypto, credit, lending, insurance, or debt.
- Health, wellness, medical, supplements, mental health, or clinical claims.
- Legal services or legal outcomes.
- Employment, housing, education admissions, or protected-class targeting.
- Safety-critical products.

For these categories, prefer softer language:

- "Designed to help" instead of "guarantees."
- "May help" only when evidence supports it.
- "Case study result" instead of universal promise.
- "Consult a qualified professional" where appropriate.

## CTA Honesty Check

- "Start Free Trial" must lead to a free trial.
- "Download" must provide the asset, not hide it behind an unexpected flow.
- "Book a Demo" must lead to demo scheduling.
- "Get Pricing" must provide pricing or a quote path.
- "Cancel Anytime" must be true.

## Validation Checklist

- Every number has a source or placeholder.
- Every social-proof claim is real or removed.
- Every urgency claim is true.
- Every CTA matches the destination.
- Competitor comparisons are supported.
- Regulated claims are softened or flagged.
- Claims do not target protected classes or exploit fear of harm.
- Report includes evidence gaps and assumptions.

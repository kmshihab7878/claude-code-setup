## Worked Copy Samples — Friend archetype (Casual 8 / Playful 7 / Simple 8 / Bold 5)

```
1. Homepage Headline:
   "Make work feel a little less like work."

2. Product Description Paragraph:
   "We built [Product] for the days when one more tab feels like one too many.
   Pull your tasks, your notes, and your team into one place — and finally close
   the rest."

3. Blog Post Opening:
   "If you've ever stared at a packed calendar and wondered which meeting was
   actually about you — same. Here are three ways to find out without
   ghosting your team."

4. Social Media Post:
   "Reminder: a 30-minute meeting that could've been a message *is* a message,
   if you let it."

5. Email Subject Line:
   "Quick question — got 60 seconds?"

6. CTA Button Text:
   "Try it free"

7. Error Message:
   "Hmm — that didn't work. We've made a note. Want to try again?"

8. Customer Thank You Message:
   "Thanks for sticking with us. We owe you one (and we mean that)."
```

## Worked Copy Samples — Authority archetype (Formal 3 / Serious 3 / Technical 6 / Bold 5)

```
1. Homepage Headline:
   "Decisions, backed by evidence."

2. Product Description Paragraph:
   "[Product] consolidates the data your team already trusts and presents it
   alongside the analyst guidance you would otherwise commission separately.
   Reduce time-to-decision without sacrificing rigor."

3. Blog Post Opening:
   "Three patterns recurred across the 41 engagements we reviewed this quarter.
   None is novel. Each costs more than most teams realize."

4. Social Media Post:
   "New analysis: the four metrics every operations leader should be watching
   in 2026 — and the one that consistently mispredicts."

5. Email Subject Line:
   "Your quarterly benchmark report"

6. CTA Button Text:
   "Request a briefing"

7. Error Message:
   "We were unable to complete that request. Our team has been notified."

8. Customer Thank You Message:
   "Thank you. Your account team will be in touch within one business day."
```

## Consistency Audit — Filled example

```
| Channel       | Consistency | Notes                                              |
|---------------|-------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Homepage      | Consistent  | All four dimensions land within 1 point of brand   |
| About page    | Mostly      | Slightly more formal than homepage (5/10 vs 8/10)  |
| Blog          | Inconsistent| Two recent posts read as Authority, not Friend     |
| Social media  | Consistent  | Strong Playful (7/10) match, on-brand emoji use    |
| Email         | Mostly      | Welcome series is on-brand; newsletter is drier    |
| Product pages | Consistent  | Aligns with homepage dimensions                    |

Overall Consistency Score: 7/10
```

Lead the consistency findings with the channels that pass; that way the team
arrives at the gaps already feeling momentum.

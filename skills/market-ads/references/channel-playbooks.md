# Market Ads Channel Playbooks

Use this reference for platform specs, format choices, and channel-specific ad generation details.

## Google Ads

### Responsive Search Ads

Character limits:

- Headlines: up to 15 headlines, 30 characters each.
- Descriptions: up to 4 descriptions, 90 characters each.
- Display URL path: 2 fields, 15 characters each.

Generate at least:

- 10 headlines covering brand and value prop, pain and solution, specific benefit, social proof, urgency or offer, question, how-to, comparison, feature, and action angles.
- 4 descriptions covering value proposition plus CTA, features plus benefits, social proof plus trust, and urgency plus offer detail.

### Keyword Strategy

- 10-15 high-intent keywords per ad group.
- Mix exact, phrase, and broad match where appropriate.
- Include 10-20 negative keywords to exclude irrelevant traffic.
- Organize campaigns into 3-5 ad groups by theme.

### Performance Max

- Asset groups organized by audience segment.
- Headline variations: 15 short and 5 long.
- Description variations: 5.
- Image specs: 1200x1200 square, 1200x628 landscape, 960x1200 portrait.
- Video assets: 10-30 seconds recommended.
- Audience signals: custom segments, in-market, affinity.

## Meta Ads

### Formats and Specs

| Format | Placement | Image Spec | Video Spec | Text Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single Image | Feed, Stories, Reels | 1080x1080 feed, 1080x1920 stories | N/A | Primary 125 chars, headline 40, description 30 |
| Video | Feed, Stories, Reels | N/A | 1080x1080 or 1080x1920, under 240 min | Same as image |
| Carousel | Feed, Stories | 1080x1080 per card, 2-10 cards | 1080x1080, under 240 min | Same as image |
| Collection | Feed | 1200x628 cover | 1200x628 cover | Same as image |

For each ad concept:

- Primary text: short, medium, and long variants.
- Headlines: 5 variants.
- Descriptions: 3 variants.
- CTA button chosen from Learn More, Sign Up, Shop Now, Get Offer, Book Now, Download, or Contact Us.

## LinkedIn Ads

### Formats

- Sponsored Content: single image, video, carousel.
- Message Ads.
- Text Ads.
- Conversation Ads.
- Document Ads.

### Character Limits

- Sponsored Content: intro text 600 characters, headline 200 characters.
- Message Ads: subject 60 characters, body 1,500 characters.
- Text Ads: headline 25 characters, description 75 characters.

### Copy Angles

- Professional development: "Level up your [skill]".
- Industry insight: "[Industry] is changing. Here's how to stay ahead."
- ROI-focused: "Companies using [product] see [X]% improvement in [metric]."
- Peer comparison: "Your competitors are already using [approach]. Are you?"
- Thought leadership: "[Report/whitepaper] reveals [surprising finding]."

### Targeting Options

- Job title targeting.
- Company size.
- Industry.
- Seniority level.
- Skills and interests.
- Matched audiences such as website retargeting and email lists.
- Lookalike audiences.

## TikTok Ads

### Formats

- In-Feed Ads.
- TopView.
- Branded Hashtag Challenge.
- Spark Ads.

### Specs

- Video: 9:16 vertical, 5-60 seconds; 9-15 seconds is often optimal.
- Resolution: 720x1280 minimum.
- File size: up to 500 MB.
- Ad text: 100 characters.
- CTA buttons: Learn More, Shop Now, Sign Up, Download, Contact Us.

### Creative Principles

- The first 3 seconds determine watch rate.
- Native aesthetic often outperforms polished ads.
- Use trend-aware sounds and music only when brand-safe and licensed.
- Add text overlays for sound-off viewing.
- Face-to-camera content often beats product-only shots.
- Keep pacing fast with clear cuts and visible product proof.

### Script Template

```text
[0-3 sec] HOOK: "Wait, you're still doing [old way]?"
[3-10 sec] PROBLEM: Show the frustration or pain point visually.
[10-20 sec] SOLUTION: Introduce product with quick demo.
[20-25 sec] PROOF: Flash testimonial, number, or result.
[25-30 sec] CTA: "Click to try free" or platform-appropriate CTA.
```

## Twitter/X Ads

### Formats

- Promoted posts with text, image, video, or carousel.
- Follower ads.
- Amplify video pre-roll.

### Limits and Style

- Post text: 280 characters; 100-150 often performs best.
- Image: 1200x675 or 1080x1080.
- Video: up to 2:20; 6-15 seconds is often optimal.
- Use conversational copy, not corporate phrasing.
- Prefer hot take plus solution, thread-style education, and timely brand angles.

# Market Ads Strategy Workflow Reference

This reference holds the detailed strategy workflow for `market-ads`. Keep `SKILL.md` as the operating contract and load this file only when deeper campaign planning detail is needed.

## Campaign Foundation

Before writing ads, establish:

| Context Element | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Product or service | URL analysis | Core of all messaging |
| Price point | Pricing page or brief | Determines funnel depth and offer framing |
| Target audience | Site copy and user input | Audience targeting parameters |
| Unique selling proposition | Homepage and features | Primary differentiation |
| Conversion action | CTAs on site | What ads should drive toward |
| Social proof | Testimonials and numbers | Trust elements for ad copy |
| Objections | FAQ and competitor review | Objection-handling ad angles |
| Competitors | Industry knowledge and user input | Competitive positioning angles |

## Objective Mapping

Map the business goal to the right campaign objective:

| Business Goal | Campaign Objective | Primary Platform | Ad Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand awareness | Reach or impressions | Meta, YouTube, TikTok | Video, display |
| Lead generation | Lead gen or conversions | Meta, LinkedIn, Google | Lead forms, landing pages |
| Trial signups | Conversions | Google, Meta, LinkedIn | Search, landing pages |
| E-commerce sales | Sales or ROAS | Google Shopping, Meta, TikTok | Shopping, carousel |
| App installs | App install | Meta, Google, TikTok | App install ads |
| Event registration | Conversions | Meta, LinkedIn | Event ads, landing pages |
| Content promotion | Engagement or traffic | Meta, Twitter/X, LinkedIn | Boosted posts, video |

## Retargeting Funnel

Use a three-stage funnel when the site has enough traffic or audience data.

```text
STAGE 1: AWARENESS (Cold Audience)
  Audience: Lookalikes, interest-based, broad targeting
  Goal: Introduce the brand and value proposition
  Ad Type: Educational content, how-to videos, thought leadership
  Budget: 40% of total ad spend
  Metrics: CPM, reach, video view rate, landing page views

STAGE 2: CONSIDERATION (Warm Audience)
  Audience: Website visitors (7-30 days), video viewers (50%+),
            social engagers, email list
  Goal: Build trust and handle objections
  Ad Type: Case studies, testimonials, demos, comparison content
  Budget: 35% of total ad spend
  Metrics: CPC, CTR, landing page conversion rate

STAGE 3: CONVERSION (Hot Audience)
  Audience: Cart abandoners, pricing page visitors, trial users,
            high-intent page visitors
  Goal: Drive the final conversion action
  Ad Type: Direct offer, urgency, guarantee, limited-time discount
  Budget: 25% of total ad spend
  Metrics: CPA, ROAS, conversion rate
```

## Landing Page Alignment

For each campaign, check:

- Does the landing page headline match the ad promise?
- Does the page deliver the offer or proof used in the ad?
- Is the CTA consistent from ad to page?
- Is the visual style consistent between ad and page?
- Is the page mobile-optimized, especially for social ads?
- Does the page load quickly enough for paid traffic?
- Is there one clear conversion action?

Rate message match from 1 to 10. Flag any score below 7.

## Testing Framework

Test priority order:

1. Audience.
2. Offer.
3. Creative concept.
4. Headline.
5. Body copy.
6. CTA.

Testing rules:

- Test one variable at a time.
- Run tests for at least 3-5 days or 1,000 impressions per variant.
- Use 95% statistical significance where sample size supports it.
- Pause underperformers at 2x the target CPA.
- Scale winners gradually, such as 20% budget increments.

# Marketing Report Action Plan and Roadmap Reference

This reference contains action plan tiers and the 30-60-90 roadmap model.

## Prioritized Action Plan

Organize recommendations into three tiers.

### Quick Wins: This Week

High impact, low effort changes that can be implemented in 1-5 business days.

```text
- [ ] [Action item]: [Specific description]
  - Impact: [HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW]
  - Effort: [1-5 hours]
  - Expected Result: [Specific outcome]
  - Revenue Impact: [$X/month estimated]
```

### Medium-Term: This Month

Moderate impact and moderate effort. These usually require 1-4 weeks.

Include:

- Owner or responsible function when known.
- Dependencies.
- Expected result.
- Measurement method.
- Confidence level.

### Strategic: This Quarter

High impact and higher effort. These are foundational changes that require planning and sustained work.

Include:

- Business case.
- Required resources.
- Primary risks.
- Milestones.
- Success metrics.

## 30-60-90 Day Roadmap

### Days 1-30: Foundation and Quick Wins

- Week 1: Implement quick wins from the action plan.
- Week 2: Set up tracking and analytics baseline.
- Week 3: Begin medium-term improvements.
- Week 4: First performance review and adjustment.

### Days 31-60: Growth and Optimization

- Week 5-6: Launch core campaign improvements.
- Week 7: Begin A/B testing program.
- Week 8: Implement content strategy improvements.

### Days 61-90: Scale and Expand

- Week 9-10: Scale what is working and stop what is not.
- Week 11: Expand to new channels or campaigns.
- Week 12: Run comprehensive review and update next-quarter strategy.

## Action Item Quality Bar

Every recommendation should include:

- Specific action.
- Evidence or reason.
- Expected result.
- Priority.
- Effort.
- Impact.
- Confidence.
- Measurement method.

Avoid vague recommendations such as "improve SEO" or "post more content".

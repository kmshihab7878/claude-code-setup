# PMM Operating Reference

Purpose: PMM KPIs, monthly rhythm, proactive triggers, output artifacts, communication standard, and related skill routing.

## PMM KPIs

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Product adoption | >40% in 90 days | Feature usage after launch |
| Win rate | >30% competitive | Deals won vs competitors |
| Sales velocity | Improvement over baseline | Days from SQL to close |
| Deal size | Improvement over baseline | Average contract value |
| Launch pipeline | Positive ROMI | Pipeline to marketing spend |

Replace targets with user-specific baselines when available.

## Monthly Rhythm

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Review metrics, update battlecards |
| 2 | Create assets, publish content |
| 3 | Support launches, optimize campaigns |
| 4 | Monthly report, plan next month |

## Proactive Triggers

- No documented positioning: marketing becomes guesswork.
- Messaging differs across channels: buyers receive an inconsistent story.
- No ICP defined: targeting is too broad.
- Competitor repositioning: review positioning and battlecards.

## Output Artifacts

| Request | Output |
|---|---|
| Position my product | Positioning framework using the PMM method |
| GTM strategy | Go-to-market plan with channels, messaging, and timeline |
| Competitive positioning | Positioning map with gaps and opportunities |

## Communication Standard

- Self-verify source attribution, assumptions, and confidence.
- Output format: Bottom Line -> What with confidence -> Why -> How to Act.
- Tag findings: verified, medium-confidence, or assumed.

## Related Skills

- `marketing-context`: foundational positioning context.
- `launch-strategy`: launch execution.
- `competitive-intel`: strategic competitive intelligence.
- `cmo-advisor`: budget and growth model decisions.

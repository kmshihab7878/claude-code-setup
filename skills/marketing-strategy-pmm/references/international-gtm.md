# International GTM

Purpose: International expansion workflow, market priority template, and localization checklist.

## International Expansion Workflow

1. Validate market demand through inbound leads, TAM analysis, and buyer interviews.
2. Localize website, pricing, and legal requirements.
3. Establish sales coverage through hires, partners, or agencies.
4. Adapt messaging for local market and cultural fit.
5. Build local partnerships and references.
6. Launch localized campaigns.
7. Monitor CAC and conversion by market.
8. Validate with paying customers and retention, not just traffic.

## Localization Checklist

- [ ] Professional website translation.
- [ ] Currency and pricing localized.
- [ ] Local contact and support model.
- [ ] Legal and privacy compliance reviewed.
- [ ] Local payment methods considered.
- [ ] Sales coverage during local hours.

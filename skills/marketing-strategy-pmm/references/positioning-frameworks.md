# ICP and Positioning Frameworks

Purpose: Detailed ICP definition, positioning development, and messaging hierarchy templates for PMM work.

## ICP Definition Workflow

1. Analyze existing customers, especially the top 20% by LTV.
2. Identify common firmographics: size, industry, revenue.
3. Map technographics: tools, maturity, integrations.
4. Document psychographics: pain level, motivation, risk tolerance.
5. Define 3-5 buyer personas.
6. Validate against sales cycle and churn data.
7. Score prospects A/B/C/D based on ICP fit.
8. Validate that A-fit customers have lower churn and faster close.

## Firmographics Template

| Dimension | Target Range | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Employees | 50-5000 | Example segment range |
| Revenue | $5M-$500M | Budget availability hypothesis |
| Industry | SaaS, Tech, Services | Product fit hypothesis |
| Geography | US, UK, DACH | Market-priority hypothesis |
| Funding | Seed to Growth | Adoption-readiness hypothesis |

## Buyer Personas

| Persona | Title | Goals | Messaging |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic Buyer | VP, Director, Head of Department | ROI, productivity, cost reduction | Business outcomes, ROI, case studies |
| Technical Buyer | Engineer, Architect, Tech Lead | Technical fit, easy integration | Architecture, security, documentation |
| User/Champion | Manager, Team Lead, Power User | Easier work, quick wins | UX, ease of use, time savings |

## ICP Validation Checklist

- [ ] 5+ paying customers match this profile.
- [ ] Fastest sales cycles are below median.
- [ ] LTV is above median.
- [ ] Churn is below target.
- [ ] Product engagement is strong.
- [ ] Customers are willing to provide proof or case studies.

## Positioning Development

1. List direct, adjacent, and status quo alternatives.
2. Isolate unique attributes.
3. Map attributes to customer value.
4. Define best-fit customers.
5. Choose market category.
6. Layer on relevant timing trends.
7. Test with customer interviews.
8. Validate when customers describe value unprompted.

## Positioning Statement Template

```text
FOR [target customer]
WHO [statement of need]
THE [product] IS A [category]
THAT [key benefit]
UNLIKE [competitive alternative]
OUR PRODUCT [primary differentiation]
```

## Value Proposition Formula

```text
[Product] helps [Target Customer] [Achieve Goal] by [Unique Approach]
```

Example:

```text
<Product> helps mid-market SaaS teams ship faster by automating project workflows with AI.
```

## Messaging Hierarchy

| Level | Content | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | 5-7 words | Ship faster with AI automation |
| Subhead | One sentence | Automate workflows so teams focus on what matters |
| Benefits | 3-4 bullets | Speed, quality, collaboration, cost |
| Features | Supporting evidence | AI automation -> time saved |
| Proof | Social proof | Customer logos, stats, case studies |

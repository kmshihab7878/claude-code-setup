# Product Launch Checklists

Purpose: Launch tiering, tier 1 workflow, launch day checklist, and launch metrics.

## Launch Tiers

| Tier | Scope | Prep Time | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | New product or major feature | 6-8 weeks | Larger campaign budget |
| 2 | Significant feature or integration | 3-4 weeks | Moderate campaign budget |
| 3 | Small improvement | 1 week | Lightweight campaign budget |

## Tier 1 Launch Workflow

1. Kickoff with Product, Marketing, Sales, and CS.
2. Define goals: pipeline, MQLs, press, adoption, or retention.
3. Develop positioning and messaging.
4. Create sales enablement: deck, demo, battlecard.
5. Build campaign assets: landing page, emails, ads.
6. Train Sales and CS.
7. Execute launch day: press, email, ads, outbound, in-app.
8. Monitor and optimize for 30 days.
9. Validate progress against the goal by week 2.

## Launch Day Checklist

- [ ] Press release or announcement distributed, if applicable.
- [ ] Email announcement sent.
- [ ] Social posts live.
- [ ] Paid ads at planned budget, if applicable.
- [ ] Sales outbound launched.
- [ ] In-app notification active, if applicable.
- [ ] Metrics monitored on launch day.

## Launch Metrics

| Area | Leading | Lagging |
|---|---|---|
| Traffic | Landing page visitors | Search/social lift |
| Engagement | Demo requests, signups | Feature adoption |
| Pipeline | MQLs generated | SQLs and qualified pipeline |
| Revenue | Qualified opportunities | Closed deals and revenue |

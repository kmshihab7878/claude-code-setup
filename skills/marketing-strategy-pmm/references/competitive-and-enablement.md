# Competitive Intelligence and Sales Enablement

Purpose: Competitive intelligence workflow, battlecard template, win/loss analysis, and sales enablement structures.

## Competitive Intelligence Workflow

1. Identify tier 1 direct competitors, tier 2 adjacent solutions, and tier 3 status quo.
2. Evaluate competitor products when legally and practically available.
3. Monitor public websites, pricing, and messaging.
4. Analyze approved sales-call insights for competitor mentions.
5. Read public review sites for pros and cons.
6. Track public job postings as possible roadmap signals.
7. Update battlecards monthly.
8. Validate usage through sales adoption in competitive deals.

## Competitive Tier Structure

| Tier | Definition | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Direct competitor, same category | Competitor A, Competitor B |
| 2 | Adjacent solution, overlapping use case | Alternative C, Alternative D |
| 3 | Status quo | Spreadsheets, manual process, in-house tools |

## Battlecard Template

```text
COMPETITOR: [Name]
OVERVIEW: Founded [year], Funding [stage], Size [employees]

POSITIONING:
- They say: "[Their claim]"
- Reality: [Your assessment]

STRENGTHS:
1. [What they do well]
2. [What they do well]

WEAKNESSES:
1. [Where they fall short]
2. [Where they fall short]

OUR ADVANTAGES:
1. [Your advantage + evidence]
2. [Your advantage + evidence]

WHEN WE WIN:
- [Scenario where you win]

WHEN WE LOSE:
- [Scenario where they win]

TALK TRACK:
Objection: "[Common objection]"
Response: "[Your response]"
```

## Win/Loss Analysis

Track monthly:

- Win rate by competitor.
- Top win reasons.
- Top loss reasons.
- Action items for product, sales, and marketing.

## Sales Enablement Workflow

1. Create a visual-first sales deck.
2. Build product, competitive, and case-study one-pagers.
3. Develop a demo script with discovery.
4. Write outreach, follow-up, and closing templates.
5. Create ROI calculator if value is quantifiable.
6. Run monthly enablement calls.
7. Deliver quarterly training on positioning and competition.
8. Validate that sales uses assets in target opportunities.

## Sales Deck Structure

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Title, agenda |
| 3-4 | Company intro, problem statement |
| 5-7 | Solution, key benefits, demo |
| 8-10 | Differentiation, case study, pricing |
| 11-12 | Implementation, support, next steps |

## Demo Flow

```text
1. Intro: who we are and agenda.
2. Discovery: needs and pain points.
3. Demo: product focused on their use case.
4. Q&A: objection handling.
5. Next steps: trial, POC, or proposal.
```

## Sales-Marketing Handoff

| Handoff | Frequency | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly sync | 30 min | Win/loss, competitive updates, new assets |
| Monthly enablement | 60 min | Product updates, training |
| Quarterly review | Half-day | Results, strategy, planning |
